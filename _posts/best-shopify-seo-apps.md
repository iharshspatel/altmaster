SEO isn't just about ranking on Google anymore. In 2026, your Shopify store also needs to show up in AI-powered discovery tools like ChatGPT Shopping, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity - and that means your meta tags, schema markup, image alt text, and site speed all need to be in top shape.

The good news? You don't need to be a technical SEO expert. The apps below handle the heavy lifting - from bulk image optimization and AI-generated meta descriptions to broken link fixes, structured data, and LLMs.txt generation for AI crawlers.

Most of these apps offer a free plan. Start with one, fix your basics, and upgrade only when you need more. Installing 5+ overlapping SEO apps can actually slow your store down and create conflicting schema - so pick wisely.
