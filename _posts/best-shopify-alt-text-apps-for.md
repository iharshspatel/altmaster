Alt text isn't just an accessibility checkbox - it's how Google, Bing, and AI-powered tools like ChatGPT Shopping actually "see" your product images. Without it, every image in your store is a missed SEO opportunity. Every unlabeled photo is a product Google can't index, a color variant it can't describe, and a shopper it can't send your way.

The good news? You don't need to manually write alt text for every image. The apps below handle it automatically - from bulk AI generation and variant-aware labeling to scheduled updates and accessibility compliance.

Most of these apps offer a free plan. Start with one, fix your basics, and upgrade only when you need more. Installing too many overlapping alt text apps can create conflicts and slow your store down, so pick based on your biggest gap.

**Category:** Alt Text

AltMaster does one thing and does it exceptionally well - bulk generating structured alt text for every product and variant image in your store using a smart variable system. Instead of AI image analysis, it lets you build templates using product title, variant options, tags, vendor, and product type, then apply them across your entire catalog in minutes.

Every color, size, and style variant gets correctly labeled so Google can accurately index your images and surface them in Google Image Search.

### **Key Features**

- Bulk alt text for all products, variants, and collections at once
- Variable-based templates using product title, variant, tags, vendor, and more
- Correct labeling for color, size, and style variant images

- Handles any store size, including hundreds of products
- Page indexing and SEO score monitoring

### **Pricing**

- Free - up to 50 products
- Pro - $1.99/month (unlimited products + automatic daily sync)

**Best for:** Stores with large product catalogs that have never set image alt text, especially variant-heavy stores.
