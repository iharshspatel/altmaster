There are thousands of apps in the Shopify App Store. Most of them aren't worth your time. The ones that actually move the needle tend to be focused, fast, and solve a real problem rather than promising to do everything at once.

This list covers the best Shopify apps across six categories - SEO, marketing, sales conversion, subscriptions, orders, and store protection. These are the top Shopify apps worth having in your stack, not because they're popular, but because they earn their place.

## **Image SEO**

### [**AltMaster: AI Alt Text SEO**](https://apps.shopify.com/alt-text-optimizer/?utm_campaign=content-marketing&utm_medium=best-shopify-apps-for-incrise-performence&utm_source=blog)![AI Alt Text SEO: Shopify app listing for AltMaster showing AI-powered bulk alt text generation and image SEO tools for Shopify product images.](/blog/uploads/altmaster-ai-alt-text-seo.png)

**Rating:** New | **Pricing:** Free

If you have product images sitting in your store without ALT text, every one of them is invisible to Google. AltMaster fixes this at the catalog level - not one image at a time. It scans your full product catalog, flags every image missing ALT text, and lets you generate descriptions in bulk using either AI (which reads the actual image pixels, not just the product title) or a template system built from your product data variables like title, variant color, and product type.

The AI mode produces different descriptions for each image in a product set, so your hero shot and your detail close-up get different ALT texts rather than the same sentence repeated. The template mode is instant and free, ideal for large catalogs where consistency matters more than description variety. Either way, you preview every change before it's saved to your store, and you can undo any run or individual image afterward. It also handles bulk image filename renaming directly in Shopify, which is something no native Shopify tool covers at all.

For any store serious about image SEO and accessibility, AltMaster is one of the best Shopify apps to increase sales visibility through organic image search - and one of the few that treats ALT text as a real SEO and accessibility input rather than a checkbox.

**Best for:** Stores with large product catalogs that have never properly handled image ALT text or filenames.

A 5.0 rating from nearly 3,000 reviews makes Pandectes the clear leader for GDPR and CCPA compliance on Shopify.
